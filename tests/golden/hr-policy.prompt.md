# Draft Remote-Work Eligibility Policy Outline for Managers

## Objective
Produce a manager-facing outline for remote-work eligibility that is clear, actionable, and flagged for human HR/legal review where required.

## Context
- Use any in-workspace policy templates or process docs if present
- Audience is managers deciding eligibility, not employees reading final policy law

## Constraints
- Neutral professional tone
- Do not fabricate legal or jurisdictional claims
- Mark items that require human HR/legal review
- Confidential employee data must not be requested or invented

## Execution Steps
1. Inspect workspace for existing HR policy templates and related process docs.
2. Draft an outline covering purpose, eligibility criteria, exceptions, approval flow, and escalation.
3. Add a short "manager checklist" section.
4. List sections that require human review before publication.

## Expected Output
- Structured policy outline in markdown
- Explicit human-review list

## Validation
- Required sections present (purpose, eligibility, exceptions, approval, escalation)
- Human-review items listed
- No presentation of the draft as final legal advice

## Definition of Done
Manager-ready outline delivered with clear next human-review steps; no fabricated compliance claims.
