# Quarterly Revenue Forecast Comparison

## Objective
Build a simple, reproducible quarterly revenue forecast comparison from user-supplied inputs, with formulas and assumptions stated explicitly.

## Inputs
- Historical revenue by quarter (user-provided)
- Growth or driver assumptions (user-provided)
- Comparison horizon (e.g. next 4 quarters)

## Constraints
- Separate facts from assumptions
- Show formulas; do not invent market data
- Units and time range must be explicit
- Reproducible from stated inputs alone

## Assumptions
- List each growth/driver assumption as provided or clearly defaulted with a label

## Execution Steps
1. Confirm input schema (fields, units, quarters).
2. Define formulas for projected revenue per quarter.
3. Produce base case and at least one sensitivity (e.g. ± growth rate) if inputs allow.
4. Summarize drivers and limitations.

## Expected Output
- Table or structured lines of quarterly forecast
- Formula list and assumption list
- Short decision-oriented summary

## Validation
- Inputs and formulas stated
- Numbers reproducible from inputs
- Limitations noted when material

## Definition of Done
Decision-ready forecast artifact with traceable assumptions and reproducible calculations from the given inputs.
